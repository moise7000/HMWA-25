\subsection{Courses}
The \textbf{Course Enrollment system} enables users to discover and engage with available courses. When browsing
individual courses, authenticated users can enroll in courses that interest them through a single-click enrollment
process. The system intelligently tracks enrollment status, displaying different button states for already enrolled
courses and providing an unenroll option for users who wish to withdraw from a course. Non-authenticated users
attempting to enroll are automatically redirected to the sign-in page, ensuring secure access control.
