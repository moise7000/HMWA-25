\paragraph{\texttt{TeacherCard}}  \mbox{}\\

This component displays a card for a given teacher, including his name, a brief bio
some of his certifications. Prior to \texttt{CourseCard}, the first table of is relatively straightforward.


\begin{center}
    \begin{tabularx}{\textwidth}{|X|X|X|X|}
        \hline
        \textbf{Prop} & Type & Required & Description \\
        \hline
        \texttt{teacher} & \texttt{Teacher} & yes & A yoga teacher \\
        \hline
    \end{tabularx}
\end{center}

And more precisely

\begin{center}
    \begin{tabularx}{\textwidth}{|X|X|X|}
        \hline
        \textbf{Prop.attribute} & Type & Description \\
        \hline
        \texttt{teacher.photo} & \texttt{string | null} &  the path of  the yoga teacher's profile photo stored in Supabase\\
        \hline
        \texttt{teacher.name} & \texttt{string} & the name of the yoga teacher\\
        \hline
        \texttt{teacher.biography} & \texttt{string} & the biography of the teacher\\
        \hline
        \texttt{teacher.certificates} & \texttt{string[]} & a list of yoga certifications\\
        \hline
    \end{tabularx}
\end{center}
