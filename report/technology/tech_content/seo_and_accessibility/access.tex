\subsection{Accessibility}


\begin{itemize}
    \item\textbf{Standards compliance}: All the HTML and CSS content is accessible from any browser
            (\textit{Safari}, \textit{Firefox}, \textit{Google Chrome} ...).
    \item \textbf{Clear structure}: The content is well-structured, with distinct sections separated by effective
            spatial allocation and appropriate headings.
    \item \textbf{Language} The content is written in simple language, avoiding the use of complex or technical terms.
    \item \textbf{Coherence}: Throughout the website, pages that share similar themes are uniformly structured,
            enabling users to easily anticipate the type of content they will encounter on related topics.



\end{itemize}
