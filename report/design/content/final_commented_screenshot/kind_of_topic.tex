\subsection{Kind Topic Pages}


\paragraph{All Courses} \mbox{}\\
The \textit{Course} page is designed to allow users to browse individual yoga course details and apply for a course. It corresponds to a single course introduction page on the site, such as “Lunchtime Stretch” below. It aims to increase user interest by clearly communicating course content, instructor information and benefits of taking the course on one screen..
